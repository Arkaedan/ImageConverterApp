Privacy Policy

Simple Image Converter for Android · Effective 13 October 2026

Simple Image Converter does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything happens on your device.

Images you convert

The app only opens images that you choose, either with the system file picker or by sharing them to the app from another app. It reads those images on your device to convert them, and saves the converted copies to the Pictures/Image Converter folder on your device. Your original files are never modified.

Images are never uploaded or sent anywhere. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers your last conversion settings (output format, quality, lossless and size) so they are ready next time. These settings are stored privately in the app's own storage and contain no personal information.

If you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new device. That backup is managed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; the developer has no access to it. Uninstalling the app deletes the settings from your device.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No ads or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access
  • No sharing or selling of data with anyone

Permissions

The app does not request any permissions. Access to images is granted one file at a time through the system picker or share sheet, and saving converted images uses Android's standard media storage, which needs no permission.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and your device as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive personal information from this.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
